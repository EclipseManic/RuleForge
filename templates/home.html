A detection rule workbench

Paste a rule in the syntax your SIEM actually uses. RuleForge reads it into one shared form, shows you what it understood, and writes it back. Where it cannot represent something, it says so by name rather than dropping it.

Author

Paste a native rule. Get the graph it became, the parts RuleForge could not read, and the rule written back in its own syntax.

Tune

Structural review always. Add sample events and the behavioural half appears — measured against those events and nothing else.

Debug

Rule to logs: the search that returns the triggering events. Logs to rule: a candidate rule, with what a sample cannot tell you stated plainly.

Understand

What the rule does, step by step, what each stage costs, and every refusal built into it.

What this will not do

  • It will not guess a field mapping. Your field names pass through exactly.
  • It will not estimate a match rate from reading a rule. That needs events.
  • It will not connect to your SIEM. Nothing here is verified against your data.
  • It will not pick which difference in a log sample you meant.
