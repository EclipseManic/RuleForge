Paste a log event or an existing rule

A single event from your console works — JSON, key=value, or XML. So does a full rule in SPL, KQL, EQL, AQL, YARA-L, CQL, Sigma or Wazuh XML. The analyser extracts the predicates so you can edit rather than retype.

Why this matters

Importing is the fast path when you already know the behaviour. The analyser shows what it understood, what it could not represent, and which predicates need checking against your own schema.

Negated predicates are kept as exclusions, so an imported rule does not silently invert itself.

Before you enable

  1. Point the rule at the table or index you actually ingest.
  2. Confirm every unmapped field against a real event.
  3. Test with known good and known bad events.
  4. Tune exclusions with an analyst who knows the noise.

These outputs are starting points, not production changes. Nothing is deployed by this tool.
